Hotel Policies

Please read our policies carefully to ensure a comfortable and hassle-free stay.

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in time is from 2:00 PM onwards. Check-out time is until 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out may be subject to availability and additional charges.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made 48 hours prior to arrival will not incur any charges. Later cancellations or no-shows will be charged one night’s stay.

Pets Policy

Pets are not allowed inside the hotel premises to maintain hygiene and comfort for all guests.

Payment Policy

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and online payments. Cash payments are also accepted at the front desk.
